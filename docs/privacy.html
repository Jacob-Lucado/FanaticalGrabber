Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

FanaticalGrabber is a browser extension that helps you reveal, export and redeem game keys from your own Fanatical account.

Data the extension accesses

  • Game titles and keys shown on fanatical.com pages you open.
  • Your Steam session cookie (store.steampowered.com), only when you click "Redeem on Steam".
  • A local list of keys you've already redeemed, with status and timestamp.

How it is used

  • Keys and titles are used only to create the exports you request (TXT, CSV, JSON, Markdown, HTML, XLSX, clipboard) and to redeem keys on your own Steam account when you click Redeem.
  • The Steam session cookie is used only to send your redeem request to Steam on your behalf.

Storage and sharing

  • Exports are generated locally in your browser.
  • The redeemed-key list is stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage). Removing the extension deletes it.
  • Keys are sent to Steam (store.steampowered.com) only when you click Redeem.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any other third party. The extension has no servers, analytics or advertising, and does not sell or transfer user data.

Permissions

  • clipboardWrite: copy keys when you choose "Copy to clipboard".
  • storage: remember which keys were already redeemed.
  • cookies and store.steampowered.com access: redeem keys on your logged-in Steam account.
  • fanatical.com access: read and reveal keys on pages you open.
